# Skull King Official Game Rules

## Overview
Skull King is a trick-taking game where players bid the exact number of tricks they predict they will win each round. Battle with rivals while striving to keep your own bid afloat and seizing opportunities to sink your opponents! The pirate with the highest score at the end of the game wins and earns the title of Captain of the Seven Seas!

## Components

### Suit Cards (56 total)
- **Parrot** (Green) - 14 cards (numbered 1-14)
- **Treasure Map** (Purple) - 14 cards (numbered 1-14)  
- **Treasure Chest** (Yellow) - 14 cards (numbered 1-14)
- **Jolly Roger** (Black/Trump) - 14 cards (numbered 1-14)

### Special Cards (13 total)
- **Pirate** - 5 cards (including named pirates)
- **Tigress** - 1 card (can be played as Pirate or Escape)
- **Skull King** - 1 card
- **Mermaid** - 2 cards
- **Escape** - 5 cards (white flag cards)

### Expansion Cards (not used in basic game)
- **Loot** - 2 cards
- **Kraken** - 1 card
- **White Whale** - 1 card

## Key Terms
- **Trick**: Each player plays 1 card face-up in clockwise order. Highest card wins.
- **Round**: Consists of 1 or more tricks (number of tricks = cards dealt)
- **Suit**: The numbered cards in four colors
- **Trump**: Black-bordered Jolly Roger cards that beat other colored suits
- **Leading**: First card played sets the suit others must follow (if able)

## Setup
1. Remove expansion cards (Loot, Kraken, White Whale) for basic game
2. Distribute player aid cards and bid reminder cards to each player
3. Shuffle the deck

## Gameplay

### Dealing Cards
- **10 Rounds Total**: 
  - Round 1: Deal 1 card per player
  - Round 2: Deal 2 cards per player
  - Continue up to Round 10: Deal 10 cards per player
- Shuffle entire deck (including all previously played cards) between rounds
- **8+ Players**: Repeat card counts in final rounds to ensure equal cards

### Bidding
1. Study your hand after cards are dealt
2. Decide exact number of tricks you'll win
3. All players pound fists on table three times saying "Yo-ho-ho!"
4. On third pound, extend fingers equal to bid (closed fist = 0)
5. For bids over 5, say the number aloud
6. Use bid reminder cards to track your bid
7. Record all bids on scorepad

### Playing Tricks
1. Player left of dealer leads first trick
2. Play continues clockwise
3. **Must follow suit** if playing a numbered card (if able)
4. Special cards can be played anytime
5. Highest card wins the trick
6. Winner leads next trick
7. Continue until all cards played

### Card Rankings (Low to High)
1. **Escape** - Always loses
2. **Numbered Suits** (1-14) - Yellow, Purple, Green
3. **Trump (Black/Jolly Roger)** (1-14) - Beats all colored suits
4. **Mermaid** - Beats all numbers, loses to Pirates (except Skull King)
5. **Pirates** - Beat everything except Skull King
6. **Skull King** - Beats everything except Mermaids

### Special Card Rules

#### Following Suit
- If a suit card is led, you must play same suit if you have it
- If you don't have the led suit, play any card
- Special cards (non-numbered) can always be played

#### Leading with Special Cards
- **Escape/Tigress as Escape/Loot**: Next player sets the suit
- **Character Cards** (Mermaid/Pirate/Skull King/Tigress as Pirate): No suit to follow

#### Card Interactions
- Multiple Pirates: First played wins
- Multiple Mermaids: First played wins
- Multiple Escapes only: First played wins
- Pirate + Skull King + Mermaid: Mermaid always wins

## Scoring Systems

### The Skull King's Scoring (Classic)
Points only earned when bid is exact. If wrong, lose points.

#### Bidding 1 or More
- **Exact bid**: 20 points × tricks taken
- **Wrong bid**: -10 points × number off (no points for tricks)

#### Bidding Zero
- **Success**: 10 points × cards dealt that round
- **Failure**: -10 points × cards dealt that round

### The Rascal's Scoring (Alternative)
All players have same potential points each round (10 × cards dealt).

- **Direct Hit** (exact bid): Earn 100% of potential points
- **Glancing Blow** (off by 1): Earn 50% of potential points  
- **Complete Miss** (off by 2+): Earn 0% of potential points

#### Optional Rascal Enhancement
After bidding, choose:
- **Grapeshot** (open hand): Standard Rascal scoring
- **Cannonball** (closed fist): 15 points × cards dealt if exact, 0 if wrong

## Bonus Points
Only awarded if you make your exact bid!

### Number 14 Cards (possessed at round end)
- Standard suits (Yellow/Purple/Green) 14: **+10 points each**
- Black (Jolly Roger) 14: **+20 points**

### Character Card Captures
- Mermaid captures Pirate: **+20 points each**
- Skull King captures Pirate: **+30 points each**  
- Mermaid captures Skull King: **+40 points**

*Note: Order of play doesn't matter for bonuses - only who wins the trick*

## Two-Player Variant
- Deal 3 hands (2 players + "Graybeard's Ghost")
- Alternate who leads each round
- Graybeard always plays second (flip top card)
- Graybeard doesn't follow suit, doesn't bid, doesn't score
- When Graybeard wins, he leads next trick
- Tigress played by Graybeard counts as Escape

## Advanced Rules

### Expansion Cards

#### Kraken
- Destroys entire trick - no one wins
- Cards set aside
- Next trick led by player who would have won

#### White Whale  
- Special cards become Escapes (can't win)
- Numbered cards lose color (highest number wins regardless of suit)
- If only specials played, trick discarded (White Whale player leads)
- Kraken vs White Whale: Second played wins and sets the action

#### Loot Cards
- Forms alliance with player who captures it
- If both players make their bid: +20 bonus points each
- If you lead with Loot and all others play Escapes, no alliance/bonus

### Pirate Abilities
Win a trick with the pirate to unlock (use immediately):

- **Rosie D'Laney**: Choose any player to lead next trick
- **Bendt the Bandit**: Draw 2 cards, then discard 2
- **Rascal of Roatan**: Bet 0/10/20 points on making your bid
- **Juanita Jade**: Look at undealt cards privately
- **Harry the Giant**: Change your bid by ±1 (or keep it)

### Variable Game Lengths
- **Even Keeled**: 2 rounds each of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 cards
- **Skip to the Brawl**: 1 round each of 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 cards
- **Swift-n-Salty Skirmish**: 5 rounds of 5 cards each
- **Broadside Barrage**: 10 rounds of 10 cards each
- **Whirlpool**: 2 rounds each of 9, 7, 5, 3, 1 cards

## Winning
After all rounds, highest total score wins and becomes Captain of the Seven Seas!

## Important Reminders
- Bonus points ONLY count if you make your exact bid
- Black (Jolly Roger) is always trump
- Special cards don't have to follow suit
- Shuffle ALL cards between rounds
- When in doubt, check your player aid card!
