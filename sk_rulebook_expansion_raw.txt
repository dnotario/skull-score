GAME PLAY
First, add the extra number cards, the 
Wild 15, and the new pirate (Mary 
Thorne) to your Skull King deck. Then, 
include any or all other expansion cards 
as you like.
3THE EXPANSION CARDS
All expansion pack cards are
marked with this compass 
symbol in the lower-left 
corner, making them easy to
identify.
The expansion cards follow many of the 
same rules as the base game cards. Any 
differences are outlined here and on the 
reference cards included in the pack.
Note: With all expansion cards included, up 
to nine players can play the full ten rounds. 
For more than 9 players, play as many 
rounds as possible that allow all players to 
be dealt the same number of cards. Then 
deal that number of cards to each player to 
complete the full 10 rounds.

4NEW SUITED CARDS:
Additional Number Cards:
There is a 7,8, and 0/14 for each of the 
four suits.
7's & 8's:
Plays as normal suited cards. If multiple 
7's or 8's tie as the highest-ranked in a 
trick, the first one played wins.
Example: Kevin leads with a yellow 8. Terri, 
lacking yellow, plays a purple 12. Greg 
plays a yellow 8 from the new expansion 
cards, and Matt plays an escape. The 8's tie, 
and Kevin wins the trick since he played his 
8 first.
Capturing an expansion 7 or 8 grants 
either a +5 points (8's) or a -5 points (7's), 
but only if the player makes their bid that 
round.
50/14's
When played, the player must 
immediately declare whether the card is 
a 0 or a 14. Unlike standard 14's, it does 
not award bonus points when captured. 
If multiple 14's tie as the highest-ranked 
card, the first one played wins the trick.
Note: these are not used in 2-player play.
Wild Monkey 15
This suited card behaves like a
wild card. It can represent the
yellow, purple, or green suit,
but never black (trump).
If a trick already has a lead suit (yellow,
purple, or green), this card plays as that 
suit with a value of 15.
If there is no lead suit yet, the player 
chooses yellow, purple, or green as its 
suit when played. It cannot be declared 
as black (trump).

6If black is the lead suit, the Wild 15 loses 
to trump. No suit declaration is needed.
Note: If the White Whale is played in the 
same trick, follow base game rules. The 15 
is highest, and wins the trick.
NEW PIRATE CARD
Mary Thorne:
Plays as a normal pirate and
follows pirate hierarchy. It loses
to the Skull King, and beats the
Mermaids.
Pirate Power (if used): If Mary Thorne wins 
a trick, that player must choose a random 
card from any player's hand (including 
their own hand) without looking at it. 
That player must play the chosen card in 
the next trick, regardless of lead suit rules 
and any other card effects.

7ADDITIONAL NEW CARDS
First Mate Con:
Defeats all pirates, but loses
to Mermaids and the Skull King. 
When a player wins a trick with
him, they may use the all pirate
powers from any pirates he captures.
He does not earn bonus points for
capturing pirates. However, if the Skull 
King or a Mermaid takes him, they earn a 
30-point bonus. 
Walk the Plank:
This card does not win a trick.
When played, the player must 
remove one standard Pirate at
the end of the trick, if any are 
present. If multiple pirates are in a trick, 
the player chooses which one to remove, 
potentially changing the highest-ranking 
pirate.

8If the Skull King wins a trick where a 
pirate is removed by Walk the Plank, no 
bonus is earned for capturing that pirate, 
as it is no longer in the trick.
Spotted Stingray
Plays similar to the White Whale
card in the standard deck, but
the lowest card wins the trick. In
the case where the lowest card
is a tie, the first played wins.
Note: When multiple Sea Monsters
(Kraken, White Whale, Spotted Stingray) 
are played in a trick, the last one played 
determines the applied action.
The Last Volley
Does not win a trick. When
played in a trick, this card
requires the player to play one
additional card after all others
have played. That player will then skip

9the final trick of the round. The only ex -
ception is if a player uses this card on the 
last trick of a round.
Davy Jones' Locker
Does not win a trick. When
played, it destroys all Sea
Monsters in the trick, even
when multiple are played and
regardless of order of play. The Sea 
Monsters and Davy Jones are removed 
at the end of the trick, and the remaining 
highest-ranking card wins the trick, in 
order played. 
20 bonus points are earned for each Sea 
Monster captured by Davy Jones,
regardless of play order.

10A FEW NOTES
• Bonus points (or penalty points) are 
still only awarded to a player at the 
end of a round if they get their bid.
• Davy Jones' Locker, The Last Volley, 
The Spotted Stingray, and Walk the 
Plank are not escape cards, and don't 
act like one in a trick. If played in 
a trick where the remaining cards 
played are all escape cards, the first 
escape played will win the trick.
• When The Spotted Stingray, The Last 
Volley, Walk the Plank, or Davy Jones 
lead a trick, the lead suit selection is 
deferred to the next player.

11• In a trick where only non-winning 
special cards are played with no 
ranking cards, the trick is discarded. 
The player who led leads out the next 
trick. (Example: Player 1 plays Davy 
Jones' Locker, Player 2 plays the Spot -
ted Stingray, and Player 3 plays Walk 
the Plank. The trick is discarded, and 
Player 1 leads out the next trick
• In 2-player play, the Wild 15, Walk the 
Plank, and 0/14 cards are not used.
